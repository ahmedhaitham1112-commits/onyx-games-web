Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Onyx Games (“Onyx Games,” “we,” or “us”) operates this website and its games. This policy explains what information we handle, why we use it, and the choices you have. We are based in Egypt.

Information we collect

When you create or use an account, we handle your email address, username, display name, and sign-in session information. Google sign-in may provide your name, email address, and Google account identifier. We use this information to create and secure your account and keep you signed in.

Games may send us scores linked to your account so we can save progress and show leaderboards. We also keep game-library purchase or access rows so the games you have added remain available. “Purchases” currently records game access; virtual shards have no cash value and are not money.

How we use and share it

We use account and game information to run the site, authenticate you, sync scores, manage game access, prevent abuse, and respond to support requests. We do not sell personal data and do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Our service providers process information for us: Supabase provides account authentication and the database; Railway hosts the website and server; GitHub may deliver game files; and Google provides Google sign-in. These providers may process technical information needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy policies. Game downloads may take you to GitHub, which is outside our control.

Storage and retention

Account and game records are stored in our Supabase project. The site and API run on Railway. We keep account information, scores, and game-access records while your account is active. You can request deletion at any time through Account Settings. Sign-in sessions and device links also expire or are removed as part of normal operation; backup copies and limited operational logs may remain for a period set by our providers or needed for security and recovery.

Your choices

Use Account Settings to change your display name or delete your account. To request a copy of your personal data, ask a question, or request help with deletion, email onyxgames.contact@gmail.com. We will verify that the request is yours before responding. Account deletion removes your account and associated profile, scores, sessions, device links, and game-access records, subject to limited backups or records we must retain by law.

Age and security

Onyx Games is for people aged 13 or older. We do not knowingly allow children under 13 to create accounts. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information, contact us so we can review and delete it. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no online service can promise perfect security.

Contact

Privacy requests: onyxgames.contact@gmail.com.
