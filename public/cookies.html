Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Onyx Games uses only essential browser storage needed for sign-in and settings. We do not use advertising cookies, analytics, or tracking pixels.

What is stored

Supabase sign-in keeps your account session in your browser so you can stay signed in. The site also stores a small preference when you dismiss the cookie notice. These are used to provide the service and remember your settings, not to follow you around the web.

Your choices

You can clear site storage in your browser settings. Clearing sign-in storage will sign you out; clearing the notice preference will show the notice again. Blocking essential storage may prevent account features from working.
